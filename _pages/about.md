Hi! I'm Adam. I'm a Machine Learning Research Scientist at [Alljoined](https://www.alljoined.com/). I did my PhD at in Computational Neuroscience :brain: at [MIT](https://bcs.mit.edu/), where I was advised by [Earl K. Miller](https://ekmillerlab.mit.edu/earl-miller/) & [Ila R. Fiete](https://fietelab.mit.edu/). My thesis ["Stability and Control: Signatures of the Conscious Brain"](/assets/pdf/thesis-main.pdf) developed machine learning approaches leveraging dynamical systems theory to characterize how neural dynamics change from consciousness to anesthetic-indcued unconsciousness. I’m originally from Toronto, Canada :canada:, and did my undergraduate degree in [Applied Mathematics & Computer Engineering](https://www.queensu.ca/mathstat/undergraduate/prospective-undergraduate/mthe) at [Queen’s University](https://www.queensu.ca/).

Aside from research, I love to write & play music :musical_keyboard:, hike and be active :mountain:, and cook :carrot: :fried_egg: :leafy_green:. If you're bored and looking for some music to listen to, I'm a writer and musician for my good pal [Erez Zobary](https://open.spotify.com/artist/7CIDRraBIHz8BenhnkJGnz?si=wid3gX6HTyaSejXmHSjX8Q).
